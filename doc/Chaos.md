# CupVerse Chaos Prediction Engine™

### Dynamic FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Tree Based on Chaos Theory

## Vision

Move beyond traditional prediction models that rely solely on rankings, Elo ratings, betting odds, or historical statistics.

CupVerse will introduce a unique **Chaos Prediction Engine™**, inspired by principles from Chaos Theory, where small events can create large downstream impacts throughout the tournament.

The objective is not simply to predict the most likely champion, but to visualize how a single upset can completely reshape the World Cup knockout bracket.

This becomes a signature differentiator of CupVerse.

---

# Core Concept

## The Butterfly Effect

A seemingly insignificant event can dramatically alter tournament outcomes.

Examples:

* A draw instead of a win in Matchday 1
* A late red card
* A missed penalty
* An injury to a key player
* A goalkeeper mistake
* A surprise qualification

These events ripple through the bracket and create alternative tournament futures.
